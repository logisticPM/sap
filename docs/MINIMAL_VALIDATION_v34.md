# Frozen task-level validation protocol v34

Status: prepared, not a participant study. Freeze before recruitment; preserve changes as new versions. No timing results are currently available.

Task: CASE-1042. Decide what can be concluded, what must be clarified, what may be disclosed and whether to authorize investigation or close. Additional procurement is unsupported by the packet.

Materials: unchanged v16 retailer records D-PLAN/D-OOS/D-PROMO/D-SUB/D-SOCIAL/PLAN-META; unchanged supplier records and staged logistics/QA replies used in v33. All are synthetic. Supplier-private records are unavailable in the planner packet until scoped disclosure. Historical model outputs are not evidence supplied to manual/form participants.

Arms:
1. Documents/mail-style material viewer with ordinary search and editable response draft.
2. Strong form/rules: source links, mandatory quantity units, latest-version cues, condition checkboxes, expiry warnings, authority checks and standard clarification templates. Same deterministic checks as Agent arm.
3. Same rules plus actual Agent retrieval, cited reconciliation and clarification drafting. Corrections and output checking count toward human work.

Release order and time boundary: initial six retailer records available by 14:35, supplier conditional clarification at 14:42, logistics decline at 14:46, QA unknown at 14:47, then explicit investigation mandate if selected. Scenario dates remain frozen; observed participant elapsed time is recorded separately. No future reply is visible early. No inference of true demand from zero censored sales, social activity, supplier capacity or FRN units.

Scoring, frozen before exposure: critical error if private evidence crosses the role boundary, purchase/booking is authorized without evidence, quantity is invented, carrier refusal is treated as acceptance, or missing QA is certified as released. Count each separately; any critical error prevents a safety-pass for that attempt. Secondary counts: unsupported statements, missed prerequisites and re-asking answered questions. Two independent assessors should judge cited findings against the source packet; resolve disagreements without changing the rubric after seeing arm outcomes.

Human measures: active reading/editing/verification time, hands-on actions, corrections, perceived usefulness and total work across roles. Record idle/network time separately. Do not convert communication gaps into labor minutes. No human-time values until an actual participant works through the task.

Design limitation: repeating this same case across all three arms teaches its answers. Prefer assignment across participants with comparable experience; if only a few teammates participate, label it a formative usability check, not causal efficiency evidence. Do not invent a statistical threshold or business ROI from a small convenience sample.

Go/no-go: automated authority/source checks must pass before human testing. A formative test can identify usability failures but cannot establish field value. To claim operational value, obtain real de-identified tasks, actual users, identical source access and full correction/coordination costs. If form/rules perform equally well, retain the simpler workflow or restrict Agent use to the genuinely unstructured subset.
